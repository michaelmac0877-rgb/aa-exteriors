Who We Are

The A&A Exteriors story

A&A Exteriors began the way the best small businesses usually do — with two people who believed they could do the job better, and cared enough to prove it.

Ashley and Andrew Manes founded the company seven years ago, and it has carried their initials ever since. What started as a husband-and-wife operation has grown into a full-service outdoor living company — but the way we work hasn't changed a bit.

Andrew brought the craft. A certified arborist with a deep background in every corner of landscaping and hardscaping, Andrew has spent his career learning how outdoor spaces actually work: how soil feeds a tree, how water moves across a yard, how stone and concrete hold their line season after season. Along the way he developed his own proprietary fertilizer blends — the kind of detail that separates a yard which merely looks finished from one that keeps thriving long after the crew drives away.

Ashley brought the business. With a track record of building successful companies in the medical field, Ashley knows what it takes to run an organization that's organized, accountable, and genuinely easy to deal with. Clear communication, careful scheduling, and a standard of care borrowed from an industry where getting the details right isn't optional.

Seven years later, that combination still defines us: real craftsmanship in the field, backed by a business that answers the phone.

Michael McDaniel recently partnered into the company, joining Ashley and Andrew to help A&A Exteriors grow while keeping the hands-on standard the business was built on.

Today we serve homeowners across the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex and the greater Denver, Colorado area — two very different climates, one standard of work. We're local, we're family owned, and we're proud to build the kind of spaces our neighbors gather in.
